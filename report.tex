\begin{abstract}
% 4-6 lines max: target, setup, key result, and takeaway.
This report presents a coverage-guided fuzzing campaign of \texttt{libpng} using AFL++ in both instrumented and QEMU modes. We describe harness design, build instrumentation, seed and dictionary strategy, campaign metrics, crash triage, and attack-surface limitations. We also compare performance and coverage across fuzzing configurations, including persistent mode.
\end{abstract}
